How to read the map

  • Each square is a 2 km patch of land where a new 50 MW data center could go.
  • Color follows the legend at the bottom of the map: the left end is this state’s weakest sites, the right end its best. In light mode the best sites are the darkest blue; in dark mode they are the brightest.
  • Colors are relative within each state. To compare states, use the score numbers.
  • Grey squares are ruled out: mostly flood zone, protected land or water.
  • Orange numbered rings are the top 10 places to build a new data center, spread at least 6 km apart. Nothing is built there today.
  • Small dark squares are data centers that exist today. Compare them with where the dark blue is.
  • Click a square to see why it scored that way. If it can sell heat, orange lines show which buildings get it.
  • An orange “MWh heat” tag in the top 10 means that site’s heat would actually pay to reuse at today’s prices.
